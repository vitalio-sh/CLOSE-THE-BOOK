Key insights

Four insights drove the score: documents first, link globally, abstain, chase rare exceptions

1 Documents first

The score counts (line, invoice) pairs, so big documents — advices and batches — outweigh thousands of small lines.

2 Link globally, then match

Payment advices arrive after the cash: link everything across the period before matching.

3 Abstain rather than guess

99.82% precision on the official LIVE (r7).

4 Rare exceptions are cheap wins

Up to 2 points per exception class, however rare it is.

Source: official scoring formula; official LIVE report (r7)
